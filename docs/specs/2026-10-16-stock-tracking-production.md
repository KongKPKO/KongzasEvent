# Stock tracking and production supply

Catalog tracking (tracked / untracked) is independent of a sales channel's fulfillment source. Preorder and postorder settings are per product/phase; Campaign settings are per product. Live-day sales use existing stock. Production demand may be capped or uncapped, but never consumes that channel's physical allocation at checkout. Existing orders retain their inventory source when future settings change.

The existing recorded-total/counter model remains the compatibility contract for ready-stock transactions. Confirmed production demand owns central physical earmarks from new receipts or explicit merchant transfers. Payment confirmation alone never takes existing ready stock. Receipt50 against confirmed outstanding20 earmarks20 and leaves30 available. Partial receipts leave an explicit shortage. Earmarks are consumed once on handover/shipping, released only when unfulfilled demand is cancelled, and never restored by refunding a fulfilled order. Timed order/payment/evidence rules remain15 minutes; production unpaid demand holds cap capacity but not physical stock.

An opening count records physical units present NOW, while receipts add NEW units. Opening tracking is an explicit UUID/request-id protected transaction: keep historical sold counters in its recorded-total baseline, convert unlimited channel allocations to finite used capacity, and earmark outstanding legacy Event obligations without changing their order-item snapshots. Existing Campaign sold/held commitments remain protected; an opening count that cannot cover existing physical channel commitments is rejected rather than discarding orders. In-flight untracked live queue/POS orders must finish or cancel before opening a physical count; they have no online payment state and are never reclassified as production orders. Pre/post-order obligations can carry over. Tracking cannot be disabled after allocation/use. Otherwise an audited stop-tracking operation remains available.

Physical on hand, unallocated stock, channel quantities (including held units), paid stock awaiting Campaign fulfillment, production/legacy earmarks, shortages, unpaid demand, sold history and fulfilled history are distinct. Do not add lifetime history to current stock. Event allocation remains committed during an enabled postorder window after the physical event ends. Cancellation releases only unused/unconsumed quantities. Management can inspect recent allocation and production movements, and existing receipt history remains separate.

Authorization: configuration/opening/receipt/overview require existing owner/manager permissions. Staff stock readiness uses existing Event/Campaign fulfillment roles. Internal earmarks and events are private, with no direct client grants. Order-item sources are server-selected and immutable. Product locks serialize cap validation, receiving and allocations. Manual ready-stock transfers show a quantity preview and are retry-safe. Stale policy saves are rejected. New checkout clients send the policy they reviewed; source changes require review again, while idempotent order replays preserve their original snapshot. Promotion quote hashes include purchase/gift supply policies. POS uses a staff-only physical stock projection; setup can preview a future configured phase. Legacy Events without a dedicated catalog consume only unallocated central stock. Fulfillment fails atomically when tracked units are missing.

Migration deployment order: DB migration before frontend. Existing clients retain ready-stock APIs; their generic family editor is told to use the tracking setup for allocated untracked products. No remote changes are authorized by the implementation request. Never undo this migration by deleting stock/order history; roll forward if needed.
